About DMD

We make brands move.

DMD is a high-end audiovisual production studio based in Cali, Colombia. We create brand films, fashion editorials and campaigns for brands worldwide. Producing from Colombia lets us deliver world-class craft at a fraction of US and European rates — top-tier quality, accessible pricing.

With diverse in-house gear and eight years of experience, we handle each project end to end — from concept to final color.
